Prepared for the board. The page numbers in the foot are Word fields rather than

## Summary

Trading closed ahead of plan in every region. The one shortfall is in the west,
where two of the four planned hires had not started by the quarter end.

## Findings

| Region | Against plan | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |

| East | +1.8% | steady |
| West | −2.1% | two hires late |

## Outlook

The western gap closes as soon as the remaining two hires start, which the board
was told to expect. Nothing else in the plan depends on them.
