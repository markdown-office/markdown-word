# Findings

Trading closed ahead of plan in every region but one. The western shortfall
traces to two hires that had not started by the quarter end, and to nothing
else — the pipeline was unchanged, the bookings were unchanged, and the
comparison base is the same one used throughout this review. :footnote[The
western figure excludes two hires. It is the only figure in this report whose
denominator is not a like-for-like comparison, and it is marked as such in the
appendix.]

## What moved

| Region | Against plan | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North | +4.2% | ahead since March |
| East | +1.8% | steady |
| West | −2.1% | two hires late |

The eastern region held its margin for the fourth consecutive quarter, which is
the longest run on record for it. :footnote[Interviews conducted 4–11 October,
n=12. Seven of the twelve were with teams that had not reported a change in
volume since the previous quarter.]

## What did not

Nothing in the pricing structure changed during the period, so a variance in any
region is a volume variance unless the mix says otherwise.

# Outlook

The western gap closes as soon as the remaining two hires start, which the board
was told to expect at the last review. Nothing else in the plan depends on them.
:footnote[Subject to the two hires starting before the end of the month. If
they do not, this section is void rather than revised.]

## Both, and neither

A reader who wants the numbers and a reader who wants the reasoning are looking
at the same two pages, which is the only reason this report exists.
